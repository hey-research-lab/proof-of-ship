      Bounties on NEAR

      How it works on NEAR

      One contract, escrow.heyresearch.near, with no owner and no admin method.

      1

      Open

      create(builder, slug, min_ships, deadline, payout) with 0.01 NEAR for storage. The window starts now.

      2

      Fund

      ft_transfer_call on USDC to the escrow with {"action":"fund","id":N}. Anything else is refused and the USDC comes back.

      3

      Ship

      HEY counts the ships it first recorded inside the window, at most one of each kind a day.

      4

      Claim

      Anyone calls claim(id, attestation, signature). The escrow rebuilds the EIP-712 digest with keccak256, recovers HEY’s key with ecrecover, then pays.

      5

      Or refund

      Not claimed by the deadline plus 3 days? Each funder calls refund(id) and gets back exactly what they put in.

      Where the money goes. HEY’s signature names the builder by an EVM address. The builder is paid at the NEAR eth-implicit account of that address: 0x and its 40 hex characters in lower case, controlled by the same Ethereum wallet. The escrow registers that account with USDC if needed, so it does not have to exist yet.

      With NEAR Intents, the USDC is deposited into intents.near under that same 0x… account instead, for the builder to withdraw or swap to another chain with the same wallet.

      Anyone can claim. The recipient is fixed inside HEY’s signature, so whoever submits the claim, a funder, the builder or an agent, cannot send it anywhere else. HEY signs a count, not a verdict: it says how many ships HEY counted, never that a project is good or worth funding.

      Try it: do X, see Y

      No wallet needed until step 5.

      1. Do: press NEAR at the top of this page.See: the escrow’s own figures, read live from escrow.heyresearch.near over public NEAR RPC, and its bounties with their state from get_bounties.
      2. Do: type predict in Project on HEY and pick predict FWA.See: the payout address fills in from HEY’s tie route, with the NEAR account it pays, 0x… in lower case. When HEY’s signature for the NEAR escrow checks out, the hint turns green; if HEY is not signing for NEAR yet, the page says so and keeps Open off.
      3. Do: switch Payout to NEAR Intents.See: the preview says the USDC goes into NEAR Intents for the same 0x… account.
      4. Do: ask the escrow and HEY directly, from a terminal.See: the fixed attester, USDC, chain id 397 and domain separator; then the addresses HEY would sign for. 
        curl -s https://free.rpc.fastnear.com -H 'content-type: application/json' \
  -d '{"jsonrpc":"2.0","id":1,"method":"query","params":{"request_type":"call_function","finality":"final","account_id":"escrow.heyresearch.near","method_name":"get_config","args_base64":"e30="}}'
curl -s 'https://heyresearch.xyz/api/v1/proof-of-ship/tie?slug=predict-fwa'
      5. Do: press Connect NEAR wallet, choose Meteor, then Open bounty.See: a wallet prompt for create with 0.01 NEAR attached. Once it lands, the preview shows Fund #N with … USDC: press it and approve ft_transfer_call on USDC. The bounty appears under Bounties on NEAR with the USDC locked.
      6. Do: on a bounty whose target is met, press Release to builder.See: “HEY counts N ships” with each ship’s evidence link, and the escrow’s own recover_signer answering is_attester: true. Press Submit the claim: the USDC reaches the builder’s 0x… account, and the card reads Paid out.
      7. Do: after the deadline plus 3 days, on a bounty that was not claimed, connect the wallet you funded with.See: Take back with exactly what you locked. One press calls refund(id).

      What you're trusting on NEAR

      No owner, no admin method

      The code moves USDC only to the builder the bounty names, or back to the funder who put it in. The account’s access keys are read from NEAR when this view opens.

      HEY’s key, checked on chain

      The attester is fixed at init. A signature counts only under this escrow’s own domain (chain 397, a salt of its account id), so a Robinhood Chain or testnet signature never verifies here.

      A guardian that can only stop

      heyresearch.near can retire the escrow: claims stop for good and every funder can refund at once. It can move no USDC.
