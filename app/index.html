What you're trusting

No owner, no admin key

Nobody can move the $WIF except to the builder the bounty named, or back to the backer who put it in.

HEY signs a count, not a verdict

Ships are counted by the rule HEY uses on every page. Bot commits, dependency bumps and docs-only weeks never count.

A guardian that can only stop

If HEY's signing key were ever lost, a separate cold key can retire the escrow: claims stop, every backer gets refunds at once.
